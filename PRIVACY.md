# MossForge privacy policy

MossForge collects no data. It has no account, analytics, advertising or crash reporting, and it sends nothing
about you or your use of the app to the developer or to anyone else.

- **Prompts, images and settings stay on your Mac.** Generation, captioning, prompt enhancement, upscaling,
  training and Help all run locally.
- **Model downloads.** When you choose Get Models (or download an optional model), MossForge fetches model files
  from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). These are ordinary file downloads with no account or sign-in; Hugging Face's
  own privacy policy covers their servers.
- **Diagnostics** are opt-in, kept in memory on your Mac and never sent anywhere.
- **The optional local server** (HTTP and MCP bridge) listens on this Mac only (127.0.0.1) unless you turn on
  network access in Settings. Anything that connects to it talks to your Mac, not to the developer.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with the app update that brings the change.

Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/FiditeNemini/MossForge/issues>.
